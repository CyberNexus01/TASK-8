📊 Dashboard Analysis Insights
This README provides an overview of key insights derived from a sales performance dashboard. The findings cover sales trends, customer preferences, product performance, and geographic distribution, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

🔍 Key Insights
🏆 Market Leadership of Samsung
Samsung leads the market with the highest total sales of ₹769 Million, highlighting its dominant revenue contribution.

💳 Dominant Payment Methods
UPI and Cash each account for approximately 25–26% of transactions, reflecting strong digital adoption while maintaining traditional preferences.

📈 Seasonal Sales Fluctuations
Sales trends reveal spikes in March, May, and October/November, with slowdowns in July and September. These insights support strategic planning and inventory management.

📱 Popular Mobile Models
The iPhone SE (₹60 Million) and Galaxy Note (₹58 Million) are top performers, suggesting targeted promotions and stocking strategies for these models.

😊 Positive Customer Sentiment
A large portion of ratings are at levels 4 and 5, indicating customer satisfaction. However, the 20.8% at level 1 suggest areas needing improvement.

🗓️ Day-of-Week Sales Patterns
Monday and Saturday see peak sales activity, while Sunday records the lowest. This can help optimize staffing and promotions.

📦 Significant Apple Sales Volume
Despite Samsung’s revenue lead, Apple achieves the highest unit sales (3,932) and a strong ₹161.6 Million in total sales, indicating high product turnover.

🌍 Geographical Sales Distribution
Sales span urban centers like Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, and others, confirming a widespread market reach.

💡 Further Considerations

Investigate root causes for seasonal dips in sales.
Analyze the 1-star customer ratings for actionable improvements.
Assess average selling prices by brand/model for better pricing strategy.
Drill down into city-wise sales to optimize regional campaigns.
